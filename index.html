Little Office Game

Meet Milo, Ava, Noah, Luna and Leo in an animated office with five computers, a pet cat, a garden, a gym and mini games.

Open Little Office

Play in your browser

Explore the room, choose a worker and use the office controls to walk, work or take a break. Visit Pip the cat, enjoy the garden and gym, or play chess, tic-tac-toe, memory and other mini games.

Little Office is free to play. You do not need to download an app or sign in to explore the shared office.
